# Community conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-Hans.md) · English

Chengyin welcomes contributors across engineering, design, writing, localization,
accessibility, safety and original media production. Participation requires a safe,
professional environment.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person proposing them.
- Give specific, reproducible feedback and make room for different experience levels.
- Respect relationship-tone, accessibility and cultural-boundary preferences.
- Disclose the source and usage rights of contributed media.
- Protect private reports and remove personal data from examples and diagnostics.
- Accept maintainer decisions that enforce safety, compatibility or project scope,
  while remaining free to document a technical disagreement respectfully.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, stalking or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information or credentials.
- Submitting real-person likenesses, cloned voices or protected characters as original.
- Sexualized depictions of minors or age-ambiguous characters.
- Using companion mechanics to encourage dependency, guilt, jealousy or coercion.
- Repeated disruption, spam, impersonation or attempts to bypass security review.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions or
temporarily/permanently restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate,
documented privately when sensitive, and focused on protecting participants and the
project. Security, privacy and rights incidents should be reported through the private
channel described in `SECURITY.en.md`, not posted with exploitable details in public.

This policy applies in repositories, Discussions, issue trackers, project chat and
official events. It does not control independent forks that do not use Chengyin's
official name or community spaces.
